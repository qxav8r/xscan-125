How it works

  1. In xScan-125, Export Channels (that's also your backup).
  2. Open that CSV here. Banks start collapsed; tap a bank to edit its channels.
  3. To put a bank in order, use Sort & renumber at the top of the bank: Name A–Z or Freq low–high. Channels are renumbered from the bank's first slot with no gaps, so the scanner scans them in that order.
  4. Edit names, frequencies, mode, tone, delay, lockout (L/O) and priority (Pri). Edited rows are highlighted and counted on the bank header.
  5. Save CSV, then Import Channels in xScan-125 and pick the saved file.

Save writes the whole plan. A channel you clear (×) is saved as a blank row, so Import erases it from the scanner too. Bank on/off isn't part of the CSV; use Manage Banks in xScan-125 for that.

Names stop at 16 characters. The BC125AT tunes 25–54, 108–174, 225–380 and 400–512 MHz; anything else is flagged in amber. Only one priority channel per bank.

Everything runs in your browser: the CSV is never uploaded. The sample plan is the Portland / Columbia River example from the xScan-125 repo. File format: bc125at-tool by Costa Mesa Tech Solutions.
